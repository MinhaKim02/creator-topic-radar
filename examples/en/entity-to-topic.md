# Entity to topic

## Request

```text
Use $creator-topic-radar to investigate a household-appliance maker
whose official product page I will provide. Find a concrete change in
the past 14 days and propose up to two 60-second consumer topics.
Check creator interest and primary evidence separately. Use UTC.
Do not force an investment angle. I have not supplied content history.
```

## Expected behavior

Wait for or request the missing entity/source when it blocks discovery. Do not substitute an arbitrary company. Once provided, identify an actual event, check its date and current status, and derive a consumer question. Mark duplicate risk Unassessed until history is available. Return fewer candidates when needed and stop at selection.

## Fictional illustration

Assume a synthetic source packet says Maker A launched a toaster on May 1, published a parts-availability update on September 29, and a creator discussed it on September 30. At a synthetic cutoff of October 1, 2026, the new question could be: “Does the newly available heating element make this toaster easier to keep using?”

The May launch is old; the September parts update is the new development. Availability of a spare part does not by itself establish ease of disassembly, repair cost, independent performance, or future sales. Label the maker's statements appropriately and investigate those gaps. A consumer consequence is sufficient without a share-price claim. These invented dates and sources are not a real product recommendation or factual finding.
