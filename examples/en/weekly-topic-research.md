# Weekly topic research

## Request

```text
Use $creator-topic-radar to find up to three topics from the last seven
days for general consumers interested in repairing everyday products.
Start with creator-led YouTube videos, then verify the underlying events
with appropriate sources. My timezone is UTC. Compare with the history
below and stop at the shortlist; do not write scripts.

Past content:
- Why glued-in headphone batteries make repairs harder: adhesive and
  disassembly difficulty increase repair effort.
- Why a replaceable backpack buckle can extend a bag's useful life:
  a small failed part need not mean replacing the entire bag.
```

## Expected behavior

State the actual research cutoff and window. Inspect accessible creators and recent uploads; expand beyond one video when possible. Keep creator interest separate from factual verification and identify the event behind each question. Compare semantic overlap with the two supplied summaries; this is not a complete channel audit. Return fewer than three candidates if evidence does not support three.
